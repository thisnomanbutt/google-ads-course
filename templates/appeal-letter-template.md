Tick every box. Do not skip.

- [ ] I have **not** created a new account

## Language Rules

### ✅ Use these

- "I have reviewed the policy and understand the requirement."
- "The following changes were completed on [DATE]."
- "Evidence is available at [URL]."
- "I have introduced a process to prevent this recurring."
- "I would be grateful for a further review."

### ❌ Never use these

- "This is unfair" / "This is a mistake by Google"
- "My family depends on this account"
- "URGENT" / capital letters / multiple exclamation marks
- "We have fixed everything" (with no specifics)
- "Please sir" / "Kindly do the needful"
- Any threat, including legal threats or public complaints

## Post-Reinstatement Checklist

Once the account is back:

- [ ] Do not restart all campaigns at once — restart gradually over 3–5 days
- [ ] Re-check every ad and asset for the claim that caused the problem
- [ ] Turn off automatically created assets until you have reviewed them
- [ ] Add a monthly policy review to your calendar
- [ ] Send the client a short prevention document
- [ ] Save this filled-in letter in the client folder for future reference
