# THE 100-POINT GOOGLE ADS ACCOUNT AUDIT

*Companion to Lesson 13.4*

---

## HOW TO USE THIS

**Score each point:** ✅ Pass · 🟡 Partial · ❌ Fail · ⬜ N/A

**Account score:** `(Passes + half the Partials) ÷ (100 − N/A) × 100`

**For every Fail and Partial, estimate the monthly money impact.** Be conservative and label estimates as estimates. Findings without money attached are opinions.

**Time required:** 3–5 hours for a first full audit.

# IMPACT ESTIMATION METHODS

Use these so estimates are consistent and defensible.

| Finding type | Method |
|---|---|
| Spend outside target area | User Locations report → spend outside area × (1 − conversion share from that area) |
| Loss-making products | Their total spend − their total contribution margin |
| Missing Enhanced Conversions | Estimate 10–20% conversion recovery × margin per conversion. **Label as an estimate.** |
| Poor Quality Score | Model CPC at target QS using the Lesson 1.1 formula × monthly clicks |
| Budget-limited campaign | Lost IS (budget) % × current conversions × margin × 0.8 (discount for weaker marginal traffic) |
| Wrong Primary conversion | Compare current cost per real customer to a realistic corrected figure |
| Broken tracking | Gap between Google's conversions and the business's actual, × margin |
| Wasted search terms | Direct from the n-gram analysis — cost with zero conversions |
| Double counting | Halve the reported conversions; recalculate true cost per conversion |

⚠️ **Always state the method next to the number.** A finding you can show the workings for cannot be argued with. A finding you cannot is just a claim.
