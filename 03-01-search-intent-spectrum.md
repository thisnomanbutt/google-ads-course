## Keywords Are Not the Unit. Intent Is.

Beginners think in keywords. Professionals think in intent.

Two people type different words and want the same thing. Two people type the same words and want completely different things.

- "water filter" → could be research, could be buying, could be a school project
- "water filter price karachi" → almost certainly buying
- "how does reverse osmosis work" → learning, not buying

**Think of a shop doorway.** Some people walk past. Some look in the window. Some walk in and browse. Some walk straight to the counter with money in their hand.

Google Ads lets you choose which of these people to pay for. Most beginners pay for the window shoppers because they are cheap and plentiful.

## The Five Levels of Intent

Learn this spectrum. It is the map for every keyword decision you will make.

### Level 1: Informational — "I want to learn"

*Examples:* "how does a water filter work", "what causes tooth pain", "what is project management software"

- **Volume:** very high
- **Cost per click:** low
- **Conversion rate:** very low
- **2026 status:** ⚠️ **Heavily damaged by AI Overviews**

### Level 2: Navigational — "I want a specific place"

*Examples:* "sana water filters", "taskflow login", "dr ayesha clinic"

- **Volume:** low but valuable
- **Cost per click:** very low
- **Conversion rate:** very high
- **Note:** this is your brand traffic

### Level 3: Commercial Investigation — "I am comparing"

*Examples:* "best water filter pakistan", "RO vs UV filter", "taskflow vs asana", "top dentist lahore"

- **Volume:** medium
- **Cost per click:** medium to high
- **Conversion rate:** medium
- **Note:** these people will buy — from someone. Often within days.

### Level 4: Transactional — "I want to buy"

*Examples:* "buy water filter online", "water filter price", "book dental appointment lahore", "taskflow pricing"

- **Volume:** lower
- **Cost per click:** high
- **Conversion rate:** high
- **Note:** this is where the money is

### Level 5: Local / Urgent — "I need it now"

*Examples:* "water filter shop near me", "emergency dentist lahore open now", "plumber near me 24 hours"

- **Volume:** varies
- **Cost per click:** high
- **Conversion rate:** very high
- **Note:** the highest intent that exists

## How to Read Intent in Three Seconds

Look for these signal words.

### 🟢 Buying signals — fund these first

| Signal | Examples |
|---|---|
| Price words | price, cost, cheap, deal, offer, how much |
| Action words | buy, order, book, hire, get, download, sign up |
| Local words | near me, in Karachi, nearby, open now |
| Urgency words | today, emergency, same day, 24 hour, urgent |
| Brand + product | "philips water filter", "invisalign lahore" |
| Comparison words | best, top, vs, review, compare, alternative |

### 🔴 Warning signals — usually avoid

| Signal | Examples |
|---|---|
| Learning words | how to, what is, why does, guide, tutorial |
| DIY words | make your own, homemade, DIY, build |
| Free words | free, download free, no cost |
| Job words | jobs, salary, career, vacancy, hiring |
| Course words | course, training, certification, learn |
| Second-hand words | used, second hand, olx, refurbished |

**Rule of thumb:** if you can imagine a student, a job seeker or a competitor typing it, be careful.

## The 2026 Reality: AI Overviews Changed the Map

This is the most important update in this lesson.

**What changed.** When someone searches an informational question, Google often answers it directly with an AI-written summary. The person reads the answer and never clicks anything.

**What this means for intent levels:**

| Intent level | Effect of AI Overviews |
|---|---|
| **Level 1: Informational** | 🔴 Severely reduced. Often answered on the page. |
| **Level 2: Navigational** | 🟢 Unaffected. People still go where they intend. |
| **Level 3: Commercial** | 🟡 Partly affected. AI summarises comparisons, but people still want to see and choose. |
| **Level 4: Transactional** | 🟢 Protected. AI cannot complete a purchase for you. |
| **Level 5: Local / Urgent** | 🟢 Protected, and arguably stronger. |

### The principle to remember

> **AI can answer a question. It cannot complete a transaction.**

Any search that needs the person to *do something* — buy, book, call, visit, sign up — still needs a click. That is where your money belongs.

### The second effect: searches got longer

People now type full sentences, because they have learned they can. Instead of "water filter karachi", they type "which water filter is best for Karachi tap water without electricity".

**What this means practically:**
- Exact match keyword lists cannot cover this variety
- Broad match plus Smart Bidding handles it far better
- Your negative keyword discipline becomes more important, not less

This is the subject of Lesson 3.3.

## Where to Put Your Money: The Funding Order

Fund intent levels in this order. Only move down when the level above is at its ceiling.

**1. Level 2 — Brand (navigational).** Cheapest conversions you will ever get. Protect them.

**2. Level 5 — Local/urgent.** Highest intent that exists. Usually the best cost per customer.

**3. Level 4 — Transactional.** The core of most accounts.

**4. Level 3 — Commercial investigation.** Good, but more expensive and slower. Add when you can afford a longer feedback loop.

**5. Level 1 — Informational.** Last, and usually only with a specific strategy (see below).

### When informational keywords do make sense

There are three legitimate cases:

**1. Very high-value products with long sales cycles.** If a customer is worth $5,000, paying to enter the conversation early can work — but only if you have remarketing and a nurture process to bring them back.

**2. Building remarketing audiences cheaply.** Informational clicks are cheap. You can collect a large audience for a small budget, then remarket to them. Judge the campaign on the *audience* it built, not on direct sales.

**3. Really commercial "informational" phrases.** "How much does a dental implant cost in Lahore" looks informational because of "how much", but it is really Level 4. Read the whole phrase, not one word.

⚠️ **In every other case, informational keywords in 2026 are a way to spend money slowly.**

## The Intent Audit: Grading a Keyword List

Here is a fast process for any account.

**Step 1.** Export the keyword report with cost and conversions, last 90 days.

**Step 2.** Add a column called `Intent`. Grade every keyword 1 to 5.

**Step 3.** Make a pivot: total cost and total conversions per intent level.

**Step 4.** Read the result.

A typical unhealthy account looks like this:

| Intent level | Share of spend | Share of conversions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Informational | 38% | 4% |
| 3 — Commercial | 31% | 22% |
| 4 — Transactional | 24% | 58% |
| 5 — Local | 7% | 16% |

**Read the top row.** 38% of the budget produced 4% of the results.

That is not a bidding problem or a copywriting problem. It is an intent problem, and it is usually the biggest single opportunity in an inherited account.

## 📖 Real-World Example: Dr. Ayesha's Intent Reallocation

Dr. Ayesha's account was spending PKR 180,000 a month. Her freelancer had built a keyword list of 340 keywords, and it looked impressive.

Here is what the intent audit found.

| Intent | Example keywords | Spend | Bookings |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Informational | "why do teeth hurt", "how to whiten teeth at home", "causes of tooth decay" | PKR 71,000 | 3 |
| 3 — Commercial | "best dentist lahore", "top dental clinic" | PKR 54,000 | 11 |
| 4 — Transactional | "dental implant cost lahore", "braces price pakistan" | PKR 41,000 | 24 |
| 5 — Local | "dentist near me", "emergency dentist lahore" | PKR 14,000 | 19 |

Look carefully at the last row. **PKR 14,000 produced 19 bookings.** Cost per booking: PKR 737.

Now the first row. **PKR 71,000 produced 3 bookings.** Cost per booking: PKR 23,667.

The informational keywords were not slightly worse. They were **32 times worse.**

Why? "How to whiten teeth at home" attracts exactly the person who does not want to pay a dentist. The keyword was working perfectly. It was just working against the business.

### What she did

1. **Paused all Level 1 keywords.** Immediately freed PKR 71,000.
2. **Moved PKR 40,000 into Level 5 (local/urgent).** She had been badly underfunding her best traffic — impression share on "dentist near me" was only 22%.
3. **Moved PKR 20,000 into Level 4.**
4. **Kept PKR 11,000 for a test:** one informational campaign for "dental implant" research terms only, with remarketing attached, to see if the high-value case applied.

### Results after 6 weeks

| Metric | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly spend | PKR 180,000 | PKR 180,000 |
| Bookings | 57 | 118 |
| Cost per booking | PKR 3,158 | PKR 1,525 |

**Same budget. Double the patients.** She did not write a single new ad or change a single bid.

**And the test?** The informational implant campaign produced 2 direct bookings in 6 weeks, but its remarketing audience produced 7 more. Combined cost per booking: PKR 1,222 — actually good. She kept it, at a controlled budget, with remarketing attached.

That is the correct way to use Level 1: **as a deliberate, measured strategy with a follow-up mechanism** — never as a default.

## 📌 Key Takeaways

- Think in intent, not in keywords. Two people typing the same words can want different things.
- The five levels: informational, navigational, commercial, transactional, local/urgent.
- AI Overviews damaged informational search value. Transactional and local intent are protected.
- Fund in order: brand, local/urgent, transactional, commercial, and informational last.
- The intent audit — cost and conversions by intent level — is the fastest way to find waste in any account.

## 🎯 Expert Challenge

Build an intent-based budget model for a business.

- Grade every keyword and every search term (not just keywords) by intent level.
- For each level, calculate cost per conversion **and** cost per *customer* using close-rate data from Lesson 0.6. These often rank differently.
- Model the reallocation: if you move budget from level X to level Y, how many extra customers do you get? Use impression share data to check whether level Y can actually absorb the budget — this is the limit most people forget.
- Find the ceiling for each level. At what point does more budget stop producing more customers?
- Then design the informational strategy properly: which specific high-value research terms justify entry, what the remarketing path is, and what result would prove it worked.
