## Why This Lesson Matters Here

Most Google Ads courses are written for Western e-commerce, where everything happens on a website.

**That is not how much of the world buys.**

In Pakistan, India, the Middle East and much of Africa, a large share of buying decisions happen on a phone call. People want to ask a question, hear a human voice, and check you are real before they part with money.

| Business type | How people usually buy |
|---|---|
| Dental clinic | Phone call to ask about pain and price |
| Plumber, electrician | Phone call, usually urgent |
| Car showroom | Phone call, then a visit |
| Property | Phone call |
| Wedding services | Phone call |
| Online shop | Website — but often a call for expensive items |

⚠️ **If you send this traffic to a web form, you lose most of it.** The person wanted to talk. A form is not what they came for.

---

## The Three Ways to Get Calls

### 1. Call assets (formerly call extensions)

A phone number added underneath your normal text ad.

```
Dental Implants in Lahore — Fixed Price
─────────────────────────────────────
ayeshadental.pk/implants
Free first consultation. No hidden costs.
📞 042-XXX-XXXX          ← the call asset
```

**The person can either click through to your site, or tap the number to call.**

✅ **Use this on almost every Search campaign.** It costs nothing extra and gives people a choice.

Covered in Lesson 4.2 alongside the other assets.

### 2. Call-only ads

An ad with **no website link at all**. Tapping anywhere on the ad starts a phone call.

```
📞 Call Emergency Plumber Lahore
─────────────────────────────────────
042-XXX-XXXX
Open now. 45-minute arrival. Call today.
```

✅ **Use when:** the person needs to talk, urgently, on a phone.

### 3. Calls from your website

Someone clicks your ad, lands on your site, then phones the number on the page.

**This only counts as a conversion if you set up website call tracking.** Most beginners never do, so these calls are invisible and Google never learns from them.

---

## When to Use Call-Only Ads

**Call-only ads work brilliantly in some situations and waste money in others.**

### ✅ Strong fit

| Situation | Why |
|---|---|
| **Urgent service** | Emergency plumber, locksmith, tow truck, breakdown |
| **The answer depends on questions** | "How much is an implant?" depends on the case |
| **Your audience is mobile-first** | Most of Pakistan, most of the time |
| **Your website is weak or missing** | A call beats a bad landing page |
| **Your team closes well on the phone** | The call itself is the sales process |

### ❌ Poor fit

| Situation | Why |
|---|---|
| **Nobody answers the phone** | The worst outcome in advertising — you paid and lost them |
| **Cheap products** | A PKR 800 sale cannot pay for staff time on a call |
| **People need to see before buying** | Clothes, furniture, anything visual |
| **Long research purchases** | B2B software — they want to read first |
| **You cannot track calls** | You will never know what worked |

### The one rule that decides it

> **Do not run call-only ads unless someone will actually answer the phone.**

⚠️ **This sounds obvious. It is broken constantly.** Ads run at 9pm, or during Friday prayers, or when the one person who answers is with a patient. You pay for the call, nobody picks up, and that customer goes to a competitor — permanently.

## Building a Call-Only Campaign

### Step 1: Campaign type

Create a **Search** campaign. In the settings, choose the goal that focuses on phone calls, and select **call ads** as the ad type.

### Step 2: The ad itself

Call-only ads have fewer parts than a normal ad.

| Part | Limit | What to write |
|---|---|---|
| **Business name** | Short | Your real business name |
| **Phone number** | — | The number that will be dialled |
| **Headline 1** | 30 characters | What you do + where |
| **Headline 2** | 30 characters | Why call now |
| **Description 1** | 90 characters | The offer and reassurance |
| **Description 2** | 90 characters | Hours, speed, proof |
| **Verification URL** | — | A page on your site showing the same number |

⚠️ **The verification URL matters.** The phone number on your ad must also appear on that page. If it does not, Google may disapprove the ad. This is a common reason call ads get rejected.

### Step 3: Write for someone about to phone you

The reader is holding a phone and deciding whether to tap. Your job is to remove hesitation.

| Bad | Better |
|---|---|
| "Best plumber services" | "Emergency Plumber — 45 Min Arrival" |
| "Call us today" | "Open Now. Talk to a Plumber in 60 Seconds" |
| "Quality workmanship" | "Fixed Price Quoted on the Call" |

**Answer the three things in their head:**
1. Are you open right now?
2. How fast can you come?
3. Will you charge me a fortune?

### Step 4: The ad schedule — the most important setting

⚠️ **Set your schedule to the hours someone will genuinely answer.**

Not your opening hours. Not your ideal hours. **The hours a human will actually pick up.**

Dr. Ayesha's schedule:

| Day | Ads run | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Mon–Sat | 09:00–20:00 | Reception is staffed |
| Fri | 09:00–12:30, 14:30–20:00 | Closed for prayers |
| Sun | Off | Clinic closed |

**Her calls answered rate went from 61% to 94% from this change alone.**

### Step 5: Call reporting

Turn on **call reporting**. Google then uses a forwarding number so it can measure calls.

Then set a **minimum call length** that counts as a conversion.

| Business | Sensible minimum |
|---|---|
| Emergency service | 30 seconds |
| Dental clinic | 60 seconds |
| Car showroom | 90 seconds |
| B2B enquiry | 120 seconds |

⚠️ **This setting is what separates real enquiries from wrong numbers.** A 10-second call is someone who misdialled or hung up. Counting it as a conversion teaches Google to find you more of them.

## Tracking Calls From Your Website

This is the part almost everyone skips, and it makes a large share of results invisible.

**The problem:** someone clicks your ad, reaches your site, reads for two minutes, then taps the phone number on the page. Google sees a click, no conversion, and concludes that keyword does not work.

**The fix:** website call tracking. Google swaps the number on your page for a tracking number when the visitor arrived from an ad.

### How to set it up

1. Create a conversion action for **calls from your website** (Goals → Conversions → New)
2. Set the minimum call length
3. Add the call tracking snippet to your site, usually through GTM (Lesson 2.4)
4. Make sure your phone number is real text on the page, not a picture
5. **Test it** using the Lesson 2.5 process — click a real ad, then call the number shown

⚠️ **If your phone number is an image, nothing can track it.** Check this. It is a surprisingly common problem on cheaply-built sites.

---

## Judging Calls Honestly

A call is not a customer. Lesson 0.6 applies here exactly as it does to form fills.

### What to measure

| Level | Question |
|---|---|
| 1 | How many calls? |
| 2 | How many lasted past your minimum? |
| **3** | **How many became customers?** |
| 4 | What were those customers worth? |

**Most people stop at level 1 and wonder why cheap calls do not turn into money.**

### The listening exercise

⚠️ **Listen to twenty recorded calls.** Nothing in this course will teach you more in one hour.

You will discover some mix of:
- People asking for something you do not sell
- People asking a price and hanging up
- Your staff answering badly
- Real customers your team lost
- The three questions every caller asks, which should be in your ad

**Check your local rules on call recording before you do this**, and follow whatever notice requirements apply.

## 📖 Real-World Example: The Clinic That Was Losing Calls at 6pm

Dr. Ayesha's clinic was doing well on web bookings after Lesson 4.6. Cost per booking was PKR 1,405.

But Bilal noticed something in her numbers: **68% of her ad clicks were on mobile, and her mobile conversion rate was half her desktop rate.**

### What was happening

He looked at her landing page on a phone. It was fine — fast, clear, with a booking form.

**But people on phones did not want a form.**

He checked her call asset. It existed, but it had no schedule, so it ran whenever her ads ran: 8am to 11pm, seven days.

Her reception desk was staffed 9am to 8pm, Monday to Saturday.

### The measurement

He turned on call reporting and waited three weeks. The result:

| | Calls | Answered | Became patients |
|---|---|---|---|
| Within staffed hours | 211 | 198 (94%) | 74 |
| Outside staffed hours | **147** | **12 (8%)** | **3** |

**147 calls, 135 of them unanswered.**

Each of those was someone in pain, at night, who had chosen her clinic and got no reply. Most of them phoned a competitor next.

**The cost:** those clicks had cost about PKR 94,000 over three weeks, and produced 3 patients.

### What he built

**1. Fixed the schedule.** Call assets and a new call-only campaign restricted to genuinely staffed hours.

**2. Built a call-only campaign** for urgent searches only:

```
Campaign: Call Only | Lahore | Urgent Dental
Keywords: emergency dentist lahore, tooth pain tonight,
          dentist open now, severe toothache
Schedule: 09:00–20:00 Mon–Sat, Friday break 12:30–14:30
Minimum call length: 60 seconds
```

**3. Set the minimum call length to 60 seconds**, so wrong numbers stopped counting.

**4. Added an out-of-hours WhatsApp line** on the website for the times ads were off — cheap, and it caught people who searched at midnight.

**5. Listened to 20 calls.** Two findings:

- Almost every caller asked "will it hurt?" within the first minute. That went straight into her ad copy and landing page.
- Her receptionist was quoting a price range over the phone and losing people. They changed the script to book a free consultation first.

### Results at 10 weeks

| Metric | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly ad spend | PKR 122,500 | PKR 122,500 |
| Calls received | 478 | 302 |
| **Calls answered** | 61% | **94%** |
| Calls over 60 seconds | Not measured | 218 |
| **New patients from calls** | 77 | **149** |
| Cost per patient from calls | PKR 1,590 | **PKR 822** |

**Fewer calls. Nearly double the patients.**

### The pattern again

This is the same shape you have seen throughout the course. **The headline number got worse — 478 calls fell to 302.** A dashboard-watcher would call that a failure.

But those 176 lost calls were mostly ringing an empty room at 10pm. Removing them freed the budget to buy calls someone could actually answer.

## 📊 Mastery Score

**Level: Intermediate**

Call campaigns are simple to build and easy to run badly. The skill is in the schedule, the minimum call length, and actually listening.

---

## 📌 Key Takeaways

- Three ways to get calls: call assets on normal ads, call-only ads, and calls from your website.
- Never run call ads at hours when nobody will answer. An unanswered call is worse than no click.
- Set a minimum call length so wrong numbers stop counting as conversions.
- Website call tracking is skipped by most advertisers, which makes a large share of their results invisible.
- Listen to twenty real calls. It teaches you more in an hour than any report.

## 💪 Practical Exercise (35 minutes)

Check whether a business is losing calls.

1. Find out the exact hours a human answers the phone. Not opening hours — answering hours.
2. Compare that to the ad schedule. Write down any gap.
3. Check whether call reporting is on, and what the minimum call length is set to.
4. Open the business website on a phone. Is the number tappable text, or an image?
5. Call the number yourself, outside the staffed hours. See what happens.
6. Write down what you found, and what one change would recover the most money.

## 🎯 Expert Challenge

Build a complete call measurement system.

- Map every route a call can arrive by: call-only ads, call assets, the website number, WhatsApp, Google Business Profile. Work out which of them you can currently measure, and which are invisible.
- Set a minimum call length derived from real data, not a guess: pull the length of twenty calls that became customers and twenty that did not, and find where the line sits.
- Design the out-of-hours plan. Every hour your ads are off is demand going to a competitor. Decide whether that is answered by WhatsApp, a call-back form, or an answering service, and cost it out.
- Listen to twenty calls and build two documents: the **three questions every caller asks** (these belong in your ad copy and landing page), and the **points where your team loses people** (this is a training problem, not an advertising one).
- Then feed the outcome back to Google using offline conversion upload (Lesson 7.4), so the bidding learns which calls turned into money rather than which calls simply happened.

**That last step is what separates an intermediate practitioner from an expert one.** Most people optimise for calls. Very few optimise for calls that became customers.
