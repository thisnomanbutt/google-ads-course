## The Order Matters More Than Anything

This course does something unusual. **It does not open Google Ads until Lesson 1.0.**

The first seven lessons are about your business: what a customer is worth, what you can afford to pay, and whether your offer is any good.

### Why

Most people learn Google Ads backwards. They learn the buttons first, spend money, and only later ask whether any of it made a profit.

**By then the money is gone.**

> **Google Ads is a loudspeaker. It makes your offer louder. If the offer is weak, a louder version of it is still weak.**

Phase 0 makes sure you have something worth amplifying, and that you know what you can afford to pay for it.

⚠️ **Do not skip Phase 0.** If you do, everything after it will be guesswork dressed as skill. The course has a built-in alert that warns you when you jump ahead.

## 📖 Real-World Example: Two People Start the Same Week

Ali and Sana both started learning Google Ads in the same month.

### Ali

He opened Google Ads on day one. He was excited. He picked keywords that looked right, wrote an ad, and set a bid of PKR 40.

Nothing happened. So he raised the bid to PKR 80. Then PKR 120.

He spent PKR 61,000 in six weeks and got 14 sales. He never worked out whether those sales made money.

**They did not.** His product cost PKR 2,400 and sold for PKR 5,000, so each sale earned PKR 2,600. Fourteen sales earned PKR 36,400 against PKR 61,000 spent.

**He lost PKR 24,600 and thought his problem was bidding.**

### Sana

She spent her first two weeks not touching Google Ads at all.

She worked out her margin (PKR 2,600 per sale). She worked out her breakeven (she could pay up to PKR 2,600 to get one customer, and needed to pay much less to actually profit). She looked at four competitors and found something none of them said: her filter works without electricity, which matters during load-shedding.

**Then** she opened an account.

Her first month: PKR 18,000 spent, 11 sales, PKR 1,636 per sale. Against a PKR 2,600 margin, she made a profit in month one.

### The difference

**It was not skill.** Sana knew no more about the Google Ads interface than Ali did.

**It was order.** She knew what she could afford to pay before she started paying.

**That is the whole argument for Phase 0**, and it is why the next seven lessons do not mention a single Google Ads setting.

## 🎯 Expert Challenge

Before you read another lesson, try to answer these three questions about the business you chose:

1. **What does it cost you to make or deliver one sale?** Not the price — the cost.
2. **What is the most you could pay to get one customer and still make money?**
3. **What does your business offer that the top three competitors do not?**

If you cannot answer all three, that is normal and it is exactly what Phase 0 is for.

**Write your answers down anyway, as a guess.** When you finish Lesson 0.3, come back and compare. The gap between your guess and the real numbers is the most useful thing you will learn this month.
